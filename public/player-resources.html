Use this page as your personal recruiting playbook. Stay organized, evaluate schools smartly, and communicate like a serious prospect.

4-Year Recruiting Overview

Think of recruiting as a long game: freshman year builds your foundation, sophomore year builds your visibility, junior year is your major decision-building window, and senior year is about closing strong and choosing the best long-term fit. Click each year below to see your priorities.

Freshman Sophomore Junior Senior

Freshman Year

Foundation phase

    Academic Fit

    • Major availability and transferability.
    • Academic support for athletes.
    • Graduation and career placement rates.

    Football Opportunity

    • Depth chart and realistic path to playing time.
    • Scheme fit for your position and skillset.
    • Coaching staff stability and development history.

    Environment & Lifestyle

    • Distance from home and support network.
    • Campus culture and athlete expectations.
    • City, weather, and day-to-day comfort.

    Financial & Future

    • Scholarship details and out-of-pocket cost.
    • NIL opportunity and support structure.
    • Network value beyond football.

    Do

    • Reply to coaches promptly and professionally.
    • Keep film updated and easy to access.
    • Be honest about your grades, measurables, and goals.
    • Track contacts, meetings, and campus visits.
    • Ask smart questions about fit and development.
    • Thank coaches and staff after conversations/visits.

    Do Not

    • Spam generic messages to every program.
    • Exaggerate numbers or post misleading film.
    • Ignore academics while chasing offers.
    • Ghost coaches after they invest time in you.
    • Make emotional decisions without comparing options.
    • Let social media damage your recruiting image.
