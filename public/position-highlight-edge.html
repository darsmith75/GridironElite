What college coaches want to see on your Hudl film

EDGE

Your highlight film is your resume. Every rep is an opportunity to prove you can impact the game. Show coaches you win with skill, speed, effort, and football IQ.

EDGE Highlight Film Blueprint

Core Evaluation Areas

  • EffortRelentless motor on every play.
  • TechniqueUse hands and win with skill.
  • SpeedGet off the ball and close fast.
  • Play RecognitionRead, react, and make plays.
  • ProductionMake game-changing plays.

Part 1: Highlights – What to Show

  1. 1Get-Off / First StepBeat the tackle's punch. Explode off the line. Create immediate advantage.
  2. 2Pass Rush MovesShow your best moves. Variety: speed, power, finesse. Finish the rush.
  3. 3Finishing PlaysSacks, QB hits, hurries. Strip-sacks and forced fumbles. Don't just get there — finish.
  4. 4Run DefenseSet the edge. Pursue and contain. Stop the run.
  5. 5Impact PlaysTurnovers caused. Big momentum swings. Third-down stops and goal-line stands.
  6. 6Consistency / MotorNon-stop effort all game. Hustle on every down. High-motor plays stand out.

Part 2: What Coaches Evaluate

  1. 1Alignment & StanceProper stance and alignment. Good pad level. Versatility on the line.
  2. 2Get-Off & ExplosionQuick first step. Explode into your rush. Beat the tackle out of his stance.
  3. 3Hand Usage & TechniqueUse hands to win: stab, swipe, rip, swim. Disengage from blocks. Keep blockers guessing.
  4. 4Speed to PowerConvert speed to power. Collapse the pocket. Drive through the play.
  5. 5Footwork & AnglesProper rush angles. Don't run yourself past the play. Bend and turn the corner.
  6. 6Play Recognition & IQRead formations and keys. React to run vs. pass. Anticipate and be one step ahead.
  7. 7Discipline & ContainMaintain contain. Don't get edged. Do your job every down.
  8. 8ProductionSacks, QB hits, hurries. Tackles for loss. Forced fumbles and recoveries.

Tips

  • Lead with your best plays.
  • Quality over quantity.
  • Show range: rush, run D, pursuit.
  • End with your best play.

Bonus: What Separates You

  • Relentless effort.
  • Versatility across the line.
  • High motor, every rep.
  • Leadership on and off the field.
  • Film shows who works.
Final Tip

Coaches watch thousands of players. Make your film unforgettable. Show them why you deserve a roster spot. Your film is your resume — make every rep count.
