Contact Us / Support

If you need help with your account, profile access, platform behavior, or organization setup, start with the most direct support path below.

Players

  • For recruiting profile issues, start with your coach, agent, or organization contact.
  • If you were invited to the platform, reply to the original invitation or onboarding thread for the fastest help.

Coaches / Agents

  • Use your organization administrator or platform owner as the first support channel for setup, access, or operational issues.
  • Document the page, action, and expected result when reporting bugs to speed up troubleshooting.

What To Include

  • Your role and account email
  • The page where the issue happened
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Screenshots when helpful

Email Support

Send a support message directly to our inbox. We will follow up at the email address you provide.
